Hi, I'm Andie.

Mad Scientist · Candy Distributor · Purveyor of Weird

Always experimenting, always building, always chasing the next breakthrough. My mission is simple: give people the world, and fill it with freely shared knowledge.

From creative projects to technical tinkering, everything here is driven by curiosity and a belief that information shouldn't be locked away — it should be explored, improved, and passed on. Whether solving problems, testing wild ideas, or uncovering better ways to do things, I'm constantly in the lab, turning imagination into something real.

If you're here, you're part of that journey. Every bit of support helps fuel new experiments, new discoveries, and more knowledge shared openly with anyone willing to learn.

The lab runs on curiosity and coffee. Everything here is free and always will be. But if one of Andie's candies made your day a little more interesting, a small donation keeps the experiments going.
