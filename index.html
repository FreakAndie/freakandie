👩‍🔬 About Andie
👩‍🔬

Hi, I'm Andie.

Mad Scientist · Candy Distributor · Purveyor of Weird

I build interactive things that make people go "whoa." Science, space, data, sound — anything that turns dry information into something you can feel. Then I give it away for free, because the best candy is shared candy.

I started this lab because I got tired of the internet asking me to sign up, subscribe, or pay before I could experience something cool. Freakandie.com is my answer to that. Everything here is free, forever. No account. No ads. No nonsense.

If something I made genuinely delighted you, a coffee keeps the lab running. But truly — no pressure. Just enjoy the candy.

The lab runs on curiosity and coffee. Everything here is free and always will be. But if one of Andie's candies made your day a little more interesting, a small donation keeps the experiments going.
